    The road to the platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4 / 10, one playthrough, no online trophies. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and the end of each chapter (15 slots) and never play with cheats active — cheats disable trophies and saving while they are on.

      Save strategy — read this before Chapter 1

      • Make a manual save at the start and end of every chapter. Ten saves cover the five chapters; rotate the other five during free-roam cleanup.
      • Do the Chapter 1 meditation the morning after Down to Earth (see Missables) — it is the only trophy moment that cannot be revisited.
      • After the final mission, free roam stays open and the 100% Completion checklist appears under Progress — almost everything left is still earnable then.

      Missable trophies

      Where it happens — map of Leonida

      Red star: the one moment that can be missed (Chapter 1, Vice City). Gold dots: collectible areas. No official artwork or logos are used anywhere in this guide.

      Why only one?

      Every other trophy is tied to a mission you will replay through the story, a count you can keep raising, or a place you can revisit. The meditation in Chapter 1 is the single moment the story moves past: once Chapter 1 ends, that balcony scene never comes back in that playthrough.
