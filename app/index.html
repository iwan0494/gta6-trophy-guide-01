    Roadmap to the platinum

    Four stages, about 200 hours in total. Finish the story first, then activities, then the long clean-up — the missable meditation trophy is flagged in stage 1, because it cannot be fixed later.

      Before you finish the story

      • Meditate at every spot you pass — Positive Mental Attitude is the only missable trophy.
      • Try each activity type once for Master of None.
      • Keep your save after the final mission: free roam stays open for clean-up.

      Missables

      Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else can be earned after the story in free roam.
